# Skill: UI/UX Automotive (Apple Aesthetic)

Rules for maintaining the "Surgical Mission Control" look.

## Typography
- **Headers**: SF Pro Display, Bold/Black, Uppercase, monumental sizing.
- **Data**: Monospace (JetBrains Mono), tabular numbers.

## Colors
- **Apex**: #000000 (BG), #FF2800 (Accent).
- **Mercedes**: #0B0F10 (BG), #00A19B (Teal).

## Components
- **Zero Radius**: No rounded corners on buttons or charts.
- **Glassmorphism**: 20px blur on strategy panels.
- **Charts**: Minimalist SVG, no padding, high contrast.

# Skill: Evolve (Continuous Learning)

Use this skill to extract "Instincts" from successful sessions.

## Protocol
At the end of a major feature or bug fix:
1.  **Analyze the "Win"**: What was the root cause? What specific pattern worked?
2.  **Extract Instinct**: Write a one-sentence rule for future self.
3.  **Update Repository**: Add the instinct to `.gemini/instincts.md`.

title: Penalty system — types, escalation, and steward discretion
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Articles 54-58
section: 54 — Penalties

Article 54 defines the penalty types available to stewards. In increasing severity: (1) 5-second time penalty — served during a pit stop or added to race time if no pit stop occurs; (2) 10-second time penalty — same application; (3) Drive-through penalty — must be served within 3 laps of notification by driving through the pit lane without stopping; (4) 10-second stop-go — driver stops in pit lane for 10 seconds, must be served within 3 laps; (5) Disqualification from the race; (6) Suspension from the next event. Article 54.3 lists the offences subject to mandatory penalties (e.g. jump start → stop-go). For other offences the stewards have full discretion on penalty type and magnitude. Article 55 covers the right to appeal (Art 55.5 — appeal to FIA International Court of Appeal, suspensive effect rare). Article 57 governs post-race penalties for incidents not investigated during the race. Steward consistency is frequently disputed: the same incident type receiving different penalties at different races is a common source of team protests and media controversy.

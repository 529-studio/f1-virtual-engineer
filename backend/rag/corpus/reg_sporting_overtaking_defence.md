The FIA Driving Standards Guidelines (appended to the Sporting Regulations) define acceptable defensive moves. A defending driver is allowed one change of direction to defend a position — moving a second time in the same or opposite direction to block an overtaking car constitutes an illegal move and will be penalised. "Crowding" a car off the track at the exit of a corner is permitted if the defending car is on the inside and takes the racing line. However, forcing a car off the track entirely — leaving no room for the car to remain on the track surface — is not permitted. The FIA guidelines state that at least one car width must be left between the defending car and the edge of the track when the attacking car is alongside. Key precedents: (1) Schumacher vs Barrichello at Hungary 2010 (penalty for crowding). (2) Verstappen vs Hamilton at Silverstone 2021 (10s penalty, fault contested). (3) Any incident where the attacking car was forced beyond the white line and a lasting advantage was not surrendered.

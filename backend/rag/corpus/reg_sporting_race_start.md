title: Race start procedure — formation lap, false starts, and jump starts
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Articles 36-39
section: 36 — Race start procedure
topics: [race-start, formation-lap, false-start, jump-start, grid, penalty, controversy]
---

Article 36 governs the formation lap. Drivers must follow the safety car or pace car path and not overtake unless directed by the race director. Article 36.13 prohibits any driver from stopping unnecessarily during the formation lap. Article 36.15 requires drivers to line up on the grid in the correct order. A jump start is defined under Art 36.16 as a car moving before all five red lights are extinguished. Movement is measured by the sensors on the start light gantry — a displacement of the car greater than the sensor threshold triggers a jump start investigation. Penalty is typically a 10-second stop-go penalty (Art 54.3c). If the start is aborted (Art 36.10), a new formation lap is performed. A false start (all lights going out before the sequence completes) results in the start being aborted and restarted. The line between a driver "pre-loading" their clutch and a false start is technically measured but practically contested — onboard data showing wheel speed and throttle application are used as evidence.

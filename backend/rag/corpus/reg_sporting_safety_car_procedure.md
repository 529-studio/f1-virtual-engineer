title: Safety car procedure — restart and lapped cars
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Articles 48-49
section: 48 — Safety car procedure
topics: [safety-car, restart, lapped-cars, controversy, sc-procedure, overtaking, unlapping]
---

Under Article 48.12, when the safety car is deployed, the race director may allow lapped cars to un-lap themselves by overtaking the safety car and the cars on the lead lap. This instruction is communicated to teams via the official timing system with the message "LAPPED CARS MAY NOW OVERTAKE". Cars being lapped must return to the back of the field before the safety car returns to the pit lane. Article 48.13 states that once the safety car returns to the pit lane the leader must not accelerate until the car reaches the first safety car line. All cars must maintain their position and not overtake until they have passed the first safety car line. Any car that overtakes before the line can be penalised under Article 54.3b (drive-through or time penalty). The 2021 Abu Dhabi controversy centred on whether Art 48.12 was correctly applied: only 5 of the 8 lapped cars between Verstappen and Hamilton were allowed to un-lap, and the safety car returned to the pits on the same lap — compressing the restart window in a manner many argued was not compliant with standard procedure which required all lapped cars to pass first.

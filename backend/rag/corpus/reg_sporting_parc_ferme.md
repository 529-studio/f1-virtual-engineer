title: Parc fermé — conditions and permitted work
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Articles 40-43
section: 40 — Parc fermé
topics: [parc-ferme, qualifying, setup-change, protest, legality, controversy]
---

Article 40.1 defines parc fermé as the period from when a car leaves the pit lane for qualifying until the end of the race. During parc fermé, no modifications to the car may be made except those explicitly permitted under Art 40.3. Permitted work includes: changing tyres, adjusting front and rear wing angles within specified limits (Art 40.3a), replacing brake pads and discs, changing the nose assembly if damaged, and safety-related repairs. Unpermitted changes include any modification to the floor, suspension settings (ride height, spring rates, damper settings), engine mapping changes beyond those in the homologated map, and aerodynamic setup changes other than wing angle. Any change to the car not in Art 40.3 requires FIA Technical Delegate approval and carries a sporting penalty (typically grid drop or disqualification). Parc fermé breaches often involve teams making subtle setup changes under the guise of "safety repairs" — the Technical Delegate inspects the car before and after any repair to verify the scope of work.

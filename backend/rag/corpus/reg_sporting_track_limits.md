---
id: reg-track-limits
title: Track limits — definition and penalties
source: FIA Formula 1 Sporting Regulations 2024, Articles 33 and 54
section: 33 — Track limits
topics: [track-limits, white-line, kerb, penalty, lap-time, controversy, overtaking]
---

Article 33.1 defines the track as the area between the white lines at the edge of the track surface. The kerbs are not considered part of the track. Article 33.3 states that a driver may not leave the track without a justifiable reason. Leaving the track is defined as all four wheels going beyond the white line. Lap times set while a driver left the track will be deleted under Art 33.3. For overtaking moves, Art 33.4 specifies that a driver who gains a lasting advantage by leaving the track must either give the position back immediately or face a penalty. Stewards assess whether the advantage was "lasting" — a position gained but then lost within a few corners may not be penalised. The 2023 Austin GP saw multiple lap time deletions due to T6 and T19 exits. Controversy arises when the track limits are defined inconsistently (e.g. white line enforced at some corners but not others in the same race), or when a driver overtakes another by going off the track and stewards disagree on whether the advantage was surrendered.
